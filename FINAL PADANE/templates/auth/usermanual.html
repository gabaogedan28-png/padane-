Section 1

Operating the walking stick

Here's how to power on PADANE and use its everyday features safely and confidently.

Powering on

  • Make sure the battery is sufficiently charged.
  • Press the power button.
  • Wait for the device to initialize.
  • The system connects to the cellular network and GPS.

Normal walking

Use the walking stick like a standard mobility aid. All emergency systems stay quietly on standby while the device is powered on — ready the moment you need them.

Using the flashlight

Turn it on when walking at night, entering dark environments, or navigating during a power outage. It works independently, so you can use it any time you need extra light.

Using the taser

This is a last-resort protection, only for emergencies. Use it only when:

  • Your personal safety is under immediate threat
  • Escape is not possible
  • Emergency responders have not yet arrived

Section 3

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Answers to the questions users and families ask most often.

Can PADANE be used without an internet connection?+
The walking stick needs cellular connectivity to send your GPS location and SMS notifications. The web dashboard also needs an internet connection for live monitoring.
Who receives the emergency SMS?+
The SMS is sent automatically to the guardian or emergency contact linked to your registered account.
Does the dashboard update automatically?+
Yes. Once the emergency button is pressed and communication is established, the dashboard shows your latest available location.
Can the flashlight be used without activating the emergency system?+
Yes. The flashlight works on its own and can be turned on whenever you need extra light.
Is the taser intended for everyday use?+
No. It's designed only for emergency self-defense, to be used responsibly and in line with local laws.
